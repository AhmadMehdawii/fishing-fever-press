About

Short

An incremental roguelike fishing game. Start with one tool, end with five. Draft, upgrade and stack them, then hunt 70 species across eight oceans until the whole sea is biting at once. Rivals steal your cards. Something in the dark already knows your name. One more round. Always one more round.

Longer

This is what you're working towards. Five tools in the water, the meter fills, Fever hits and the ocean loses its mind. Fish everywhere, catches every half second, gold piling up faster than you can read the numbers. It's the best ten seconds in the game.

You don't start there. You start with one tool and twenty seconds a round. One fish, a few coins, go again. Fishing Fever is built on short runs that escalate fast. Round 4 is calm. Round 20 is busy. Round 40 is chaos.

So draft, upgrade, break. Every round hands you a new tool. Rods, nets, harpoons, drones, traps, 45 of them. Upgrade them, stack them, and chase that one combo that makes the whole run click.

Then leave the coast. Eight oceans and every one of them fishes differently. Warm reefs. Arctic ice. A drowned city. Seventy species, a Fishdex to fill and an aquarium to keep them in. And every so often a Big One turns up and the whole round is about landing it.

You're not alone out there. Golden fish surface for a few seconds and vanish. From round fifty a rival boat pulls up beside you. Win the best of three or watch them sail off with one of your cards.

And somewhere out there, something is waiting. Bottles wash up with pages from a captain's log. Read them in order. Around page seven you'll want to stop. You won't.

45 tools. 70 fish. 8 oceans. And it goes deeper than you think.

What the demo has

The free demo is the first ocean: 13 tools, 22 fish, drafting, upgrades, the shop, talents, treasures and Fever mode. Boss fish, rivals, the other oceans and the captain's log are full game only.
