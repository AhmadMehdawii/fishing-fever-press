Privacy policy

This policy covers the small outreach tool Gut Punch Studio uses to email content creators about Fishing Fever. The tool is used only by Gut Punch Studio, on the studio's own Gmail account.

What it accesses. With the account owner's permission, the tool sends plain-text emails from that Gmail account and reads back messages it sent, to check they went out correctly. It does not read, store or process anyone else's mailbox.

What it stores. A login token, kept on the studio's own computer. No email content, contacts or personal data is sent to any server run by Gut Punch Studio or any third party.

Sharing. Nothing is sold or shared.

Removing access. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
